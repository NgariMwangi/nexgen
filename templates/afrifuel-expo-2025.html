Nexgen at AfriFuel Expo 2025

Our participation in Africa's premier fueling technology event

Nexgen Fuel Works was pleased to participate in this year's AfriFueling Expo, which was hosted at the Sarit Centre and brought together professionals in the industry to present and discuss the newest developments in fuelling technology. Our team demonstrated our comprehensive maintenance and technical solutions designed for the contemporary forecourt to clients, prospects, and other specialists.

Visitors got a personal look at our ProGauge fuel management systems, state-of-the-art automation solutions, and cutting-edge forecourt technologies at our interactive booth. Our professionals were available to answer questions, give live demonstrations, and create deep relationships around smarter fuel infrastructure.

Nexgen's participation in the AfriFueling Expo demonstrates its dedication to promoting quality and dependability in fuel management throughout East Africa.
